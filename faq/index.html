General
What is BAM?

BAM (Biophilic Anchor Method™) is a peer-supported, sensory-first wellness protocol. It uses living ecosystems — moss jars, bioactive terrariums, nano aquariums, and polished minerals — to regulate the nervous system through the senses. The method is structured around three daily practices: Observe, Tend, Integrate.

Is BAM a medical therapy?

No. BAM is a wellness practice, not a medical treatment. We do not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Our clinical pilots (Ecological Interoceptive Therapy™) are always conducted in partnership with licensed providers, never as a standalone medical intervention. We are transparent about this in our Ethics Charter.

How much does an ecosystem cost?

Our products range from $35 (Anchor Stone) to $550 (Bioactive Terrarium). The most popular entry point is the Moss Jar at $85–$125. See our Products page for the full catalog.

Who can use BAM?

Anyone. BAM was designed for people who want to regulate their nervous system without relying solely on willpower or talk therapy. Our composite profiles include a 14-year-old student, a 38-year-old marketing director, and a 71-year-old retired teacher. Any age. Any stage. Real change.

The Science & Research
What is "Predictive Coherence™"?

Predictive Coherence™ is the apex state of nervous system regulation in the BAM framework. Grounded in modern Predictive Processing neuroscience, it is the state where your brain's predictions (what it expects to feel) match the sensory evidence (safety, biological rhythm, nature). When this happens, the threat system powers down, dissociation dissolves, and you return to grounded presence and conscious choice.

How is this different from Polyvagal Theory?

While Polyvagal Theory popularized the concepts of "freeze" and "shutdown," recent 2026 neuroscientific consensus has heavily critiqued its anatomical claims. BAM bypasses outdated vagal "ladders" and instead builds on the much stronger, empirically validated frameworks of **Predictive Processing**, **Interoception**, and **Dan Siegel's Window of Tolerance**.

Is BAM scientifically validated?

Yes. The framework is legally registered with the Canadian Intellectual Property Office (CIPO Reg. No. 1251262) and permanently archived via Zenodo (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23674562). Furthermore, the entire methodology is being published in a rigorous 4-book academic series (2026) covering neuroscience, clinical therapy, architecture, and ecology.

Products & Services
What is the difference between a Moss Jar and a Bioactive Terrarium?

A Moss Jar is a sealed micro-ecosystem with moss, springtails, and a decorative mineral. It requires minimal maintenance (a misting every 1-2 weeks). A Bioactive Terrarium is a larger, open ecosystem with plants, substrate, isopods, and optionally a peaceful reptile like a corn snake. It requires daily observation and weekly maintenance.

Do I need to feed the animals?

Springtails and isopods feed on decaying plant matter — they are self-sustaining. If you have a reptile, you will need to feed it according to the species care guide provided with your terrarium. We provide detailed printed instructions and video support.

What if my ecosystem dies?

We offer a 30-day health guarantee on all living systems. If your ecosystem fails within 30 days due to a pre-existing issue, we will replace it. After 30 days, we provide ongoing troubleshooting support through the Anchor Club community.

Do you offer in-home setup?

Yes. In-home setup is available in Simcoe County and the Greater Toronto Area starting at $250. A Certified Wellness Architect will deliver, install, and teach you the maintenance protocol. Book a consultation to learn more.

We partner with therapists, rehabilitation centres, and clinical facilities to run 90-day pilots. We provide the ecosystems, training, and evaluation framework. You provide the participants and clinical oversight. Outcomes are measured using the GAD-7 (anxiety) and MAIA-2 (interoceptive awareness) scales. See the Clinical page for details.
