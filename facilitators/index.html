The Opportunity

A self-funded small business with a proven method.

You don't need to be a therapist to deliver BAM. You need to be a trained, ethical facilitator. Once certified, you operate in your own territory, keep the majority of the revenue, and become part of a national network.

◈

Your Territory, Your Business

You are not an employee. You are a licensee. You set your own hours, run your own consultations, and deliver installations in your exclusive territory. BAM provides the brand, the method, the supply chain, and the legal backing.

◉

Closed-Loop Supply Chain

You don't need to source animals or materials. We provide a vetted network of captive breeders and ethical suppliers. You order through the co-op, and the materials arrive at your door. No inventory risk.

Why this matters: Every facilitator is a self-funded small business owner. They pay us to operate. They market themselves. They source from our network. We collect recurring revenue while they do the work. This is the structure that turns a solo founder into a category.
The 4-Week Intensive

What you will learn.

Our certification is a 4-week intensive program. Every candidate completes one paid client engagement under supervision before certification is granted.

Phase 1 · Week 1

Clinical Foundation

Understanding the nervous system, the 14 senses, Attention Restoration Theory, ethics, and the screening questionnaire.

Phase 2 · Week 2

Biological Architecture

Building the ecosystems. Moss jars, bioactive terrariums, animal husbandry (springtails, isopods), and closed-loop sourcing.

Phase 3 · Week 3

Operational Logistics

Client intake, pricing strategies, legal waivers, insurance requirements, and how to run a local workshop.

Phase 4 · Week 4

Capstone Deployment

You complete one paid client engagement under the supervision of a BAM master facilitator. We review your work and certify you.
