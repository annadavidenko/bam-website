The Closed-Loop Network

Ethical sourcing. Verified genetics.

BAM is building a trusted network of captive breeders. We do not work with wild-caught specimens or mass-breeding facilities. We maintain three vetted breeders for every targeted genetic line.

◈

Ethical Sourcing

If you are a breeder who shares our ethics, we want to distribute your animals. We visit your facility, review your husbandry practices, and verify your lineage records. Once approved, you become a preferred supplier to our national facilitator network.

◉

Verified Genetics

Every animal placed through BAM is registered. We are building the BAM Genetic Registry — a closed-loop database of DNA-verified lineage records. This becomes the "Carfax" of quality reptile genetics, protecting both breeders and buyers.

The win for breeders: You get access to a national network of certified facilitators who order consistently, pay on time, and treat your animals with respect. No more haggling at expos. No more no-shows.
Retail & Consignment

Bring BAM to your shelves.

We offer consignment and wholesale options for Anchor Stones, Desktop Sanctuary Jars, and Marimo Kits. High margins, low inventory risk, and a brand that brings new customers into your store.

Purchase BAM kits at wholesale pricing (40–50% off retail). We ship directly to your store. You keep the full margin. Ideal for pet stores, garden centres, and wellness boutiques.
