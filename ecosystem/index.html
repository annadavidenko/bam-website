The BAM Ecosystem

Three entities.
One mission.

BAM is not a single company. It is a circular ecosystem of three operational entities, underpinned by a foundation of registered intellectual property and academic research. Each feeds the others. None subsidizes another.

The Operational Structure

The three entities.

Each entity has its own legal structure, its own board, and its own mission. Together, they form a self-sustaining ecosystem where every dollar serves two purposes: revenue and impact.

For-Profit
Karma Strategic Intelligence Inc.

The Engine

The for-profit arm handles all revenue-generating activity, licensing the IP and funding the ecosystem through commercial operations.

  • Media (YouTube, Instagram, LinkedIn)
  • Products (Anchor Stones, Moss Jars, Terrariums)

  • B2B Licensing (E.I.T. clinical frameworks)
  • Book Sales (The 4-Book Series)

How It Works

The circular economy.

Every part of BAM feeds another part. The IP creates the framework. The for-profit generates revenue. The revenue funds the charity. The charity earns credibility. The co-op delivers at scale.

1. IP Creates the Asset

The registered methodology and books create a proprietary asset that can be licensed.

2. For-Profit Generates Revenue

Products, services, and licensing create cash flow. This is the engine.

3. Charity Earns Credibility

Clinical pilots produce data, white papers, and trust with grant officers.

4. Co-op Delivers at Scale

Certified architects expand the method into new territories, paying licensing back to the engine.

Why this matters: Most wellness companies are either for-profit or non-profit. BAM is a multi-layered structure protected by registered IP. This hybrid structure creates financial resilience, social impact, and scalability — without compromising the scientific integrity of the work.
