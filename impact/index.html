Charity & Impact

Every ecosystem
funds a free one.

The Biophilic Anchor Foundation is the charity arm of BAM. It runs free programs for vulnerable populations — recovery, seniors, and youth. Funded by grants, donations, and corporate gifts.

How Funds Are Used

Transparent allocation.

Every dollar donated to the Biophilic Anchor Foundation is allocated according to a strict formula. We publish our annual report with full financials.

60%
Programs

Free ecosystems, Anchor Stones, workshops, and library lending for recovery programs, seniors, and youth.

20%
Evaluation & Research

GAD-7 surveys, white paper publication, third-party evaluation, and clinical pilot support.

15%
Administration

Bookkeeping, insurance, software, and the operational costs of running a registered charity.

5%
Fundraising

Grant writing, donor outreach, and annual report production. Capped at 5% to maximize impact.

Current Programs

What we run right now.

These programs are active or in development. As the charity arm receives charitable status, we will expand into new regions and populations.

◈

Recovery Programs

Free Desktop Sanctuary Jars for participants in stabilization and maintenance phases of recovery. Distributed through partner rehabilitation centres and community organizations.

◎

Senior Programs

Free low-maintenance ecosystems and Anchor Stone sets for seniors in assisted living and community care. Designed for accessibility and minimal upkeep.

✦

Youth Programs

Free workshops and starter kits for youth in schools, after-school programs, and community centres. Introduces nervous system regulation through hands-on ecosystem building.

◇

Clinical Pilots

90-day GAD-7 pilots with therapists, rehabilitation centres, and clinical partners. Data contributes to the BAM white paper on nervous system regulation outcomes.

◉

Library Lending

Ecosystems available for loan at partner public libraries. Patrons can borrow a Moss Jar or Anchor Stone for 30 days to experience the method before purchasing.

◆

Research & Evaluation

Pre- and post-intervention GAD-7 data collection, third-party analysis, and public white paper publication within 12 months of pilot completion.
