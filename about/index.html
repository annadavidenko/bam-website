The Founder's Journey

Anna Davidenko

Founder & Wellness Architect

Honours BCIA · NLP Master Practitioner · Founder of Karma Strategic Intelligence

Anna's background is not in wellness. It is in forensic intelligence — the study of patterns, behavioural mapping, and systems analysis. She spent years learning how to read complex systems and identify hidden structures.

She also spent years rebuilding her own nervous system after addiction. She discovered that traditional talk therapy, while essential, was not enough. Her body needed a physical anchor. She found it in the quiet observation of living systems — plants, reptiles, and polished minerals.

She combined her forensic training with her lived experience and built BAM. Today, she is a single mother, a full-time student, and a mission-driven entrepreneur.

The Philosophy

Hardware vs. Willpower.

The $6.8 trillion wellness industry is built on apps, journals, and willpower. It asks people to think their way toward feeling better. But for people in recovery, for high-stress professionals, and for anyone with a dysregulated nervous system, thinking is the problem.

BAM takes a different path. We build physical, living environments that settle the nervous system through the senses — without needing willpower or motivation. You don't have to try to feel better. The space does the work.

Our core belief: The human nervous system evolved to regulate itself through contact with living systems. Modern life removed that contact. BAM puts it back — in a structured, accessible, and ethical way.
The Science

What BAM is built on.

BAM is not a wellness trend. It is a protocol built on decades of established research in neuroscience, ecology, and psychology.

◈

Attention Restoration Theory

Kaplan & Kaplan. Natural environments restore directed attention and reduce mental fatigue. BAM applies this at the micro-scale — a desktop ecosystem.

◉

Biophilia

E.O. Wilson. Humans have an innate, genetically based tendency to connect with nature and other living systems. BAM is a structured way to satisfy that need.

◎

Tactile Grounding

Kappes & Morewedge. Physical touch with natural textures (stone, moss, wood) reduces anxiety and promotes a sense of safety in the body.

◇

Perceptual Revision

Doidge, Beck. The brain can be rewired through repeated, positive sensory experiences. BAM provides a daily, low-effort protocol that builds new neural pathways.

The Journey

How BAM came to be.

BAM was not an overnight idea. It was built over years of personal recovery, academic training, and systematic development.

Phase 01
Personal Recovery. Anna rebuilt her own nervous system after addiction using living systems — terrariums, fish, reptiles, and polished minerals. The method worked for her. She began documenting it.
Phase 02
Forensic Training. She completed an Honours degree in Crime & Intelligence Analysis (BCIA) and applied her forensic pattern-recognition skills to the wellness industry. She identified a structural gap: no physical, non-verbal delivery method.
Phase 03
Method Development. She formalized the protocol: three pillars (Observe, Tend, Mirror), four ecosystem types (Desk Stone, Moss Jar, Bioactive Terrarium, Water Garden), and the 3-minute Suitability Questionnaire.
Phase 04
Business Structure. She built the hybrid model: a for-profit company (KSI Inc.), a charity arm (Biophilic Anchor Foundation, in progress), and a Certified Facilitators Co-op. The Certified Facilitator Program was designed as the growth engine.
Phase 05 · Now
Launch Mode. BAM is live at biophilic-anchor-method.com. The questionnaire prototype is working. Clinical pilots are in development. The Anchor Club community is being built. The HELIX Cohort is next.
