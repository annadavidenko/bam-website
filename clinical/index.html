The Clinical Gap

Why cognitive therapy requires physical hardware.

Standard cognitive-based talk therapy relies heavily on prefrontal cortex function. According to the Window of Tolerance model, when a patient is in a state of hyper-arousal (panic) or hypo-arousal (dissociation), cognitive processing is mechanically offline. They cannot "think" their way to a regulated state.

◈

Correcting Prediction Errors

Grounded in Predictive Processing, we classify trauma as a physiological "prediction error"—the brain expects threat even when the environment is safe. E.I.T. provides a physical, non-verbal entry point. The predictable rhythm of a living system provides the exact sensory evidence the brain requires to update its predictive model.

◉

Scaffolding for Therapy

When a patient's autonomic nervous system is mechanically anchored by a daily sensory protocol, they arrive at their clinical sessions with a lower baseline of arousal. BAM acts as physiological scaffolding, expanding the Window of Tolerance so the clinical work can actually take root.

Target Demographics: Therapists, rehabilitation centres, addiction counsellors, EAP providers, and occupational health departments seeking measurable, low-cost, non-pharmacological hardware for high-stress populations.
Pilot Structure

A 90-Day Structural Deployment.

We partner with your facility to execute a structured 90-day pilot. We provide the physical hardware (ecosystems), the protocol training, and the evaluation framework. You provide the clinical oversight and the subject cohort.

Phase 1 · Weeks 1-2

Baseline & Assessment

Subjects complete the EcoPet Suitability Questionnaire and standardized baseline metrics using the GAD-7 (anxiety) and MAIA-2 (interoceptive awareness) scales. We calibrate the appropriate BAM ecosystem for each subject and enforce hygiene protocols.

Phase 2 · Weeks 3-11

Hardware Deployment

Subjects execute the daily 1-5 minute BAM protocol (Observe, Tend, Integrate). Your clinical staff monitor weekly. We provide continuous technical support and troubleshooting for the biological systems.

Phase 3 · Week 12

Data Capture & Evaluation

Subjects complete the post-intervention GAD-7 and MAIA-2. We compile the data, generate a white paper on physiological outcomes, and present the findings to your team to determine if a permanent installation is clinically justified.

Ethics & Safety First

Rigorous screening. Closed-loop protocols.

BAM is a peer-supported wellness framework. We do not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Our clinical partnerships are built on a foundation of strict ethical boundaries, data privacy, and biological safety.

Our Clinical Commitments

1. No active crisis intervention. We do not deploy systems for individuals in active addiction or acute crisis. BAM is a complementary protocol for the stabilization and maintenance phases, or for high-stress professionals seeking preventative regulation.

2. Zoonotic & Hygiene Safety. All biological anchors are captive-bred and quarantined. We mandate strict hygiene protocols (sanitization stations, handling rules) to mitigate any risk of bacterial transmission. The safety of the patient and the biological hardware is paramount.

3. Autonomy and Choice. Every subject completes our Suitability Questionnaire. If the data indicates that a living ecosystem is not a physiological match, the intervention is not forced. Complete autonomous choice is hardcoded into the protocol.

4. Transparency. Our Ethics Charter is public. Every clinical pilot is conducted in partnership with licensed providers, never as a standalone medical intervention.
