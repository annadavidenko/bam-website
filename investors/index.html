The Scale Story

The Certified Facilitator Program

BAM grows through certification, not hiring. Each graduate becomes a licensed operator in their own territory, sourcing from our closed-loop network, delivering the same method, and paying ongoing licensing.

✦

Program Structure

4-week intensive. Four phases: Clinical Foundation, Biological Architecture, Operational Logistics, Capstone Deployment. Every candidate completes one paid client engagement under supervision before certification.

✦

Investment & Licensing

$2,500 certification tuition. $300/month ongoing regional licensing. Optional territory exclusivity at $15K–$50K. All fees are paid by the facilitator, not BAM.

✦

Year 5 Target

140 certified facilitators across Canada and the US. At $300/month each, that is $504,000 annual recurring revenue — separate from products, services, and expos.

Why this matters: Every facilitator is a self-funded small business owner. They pay us to operate. They market themselves. They source from our network. We collect recurring revenue while they do the work. This is the structure that turns a solo founder into a category.

Investor Q&A

Questions We Get Asked

These are the questions that come up in every serious conversation. Answers are here so you can pre-screen us before reaching out.

Why you? Why are you the right person to build this?

Three reasons. Forensic intelligence training lets me see hidden structures in complex systems — that is how I designed the Somatic Element Index and the BAM protocol. An Addictions diploma and NLP Master certification mean I understand how habits form and how to interrupt loops without clinical jargon. And lived experience: I rebuilt my own nervous system after addiction using the exact method I now teach. I did not read this in a textbook. I lived it.

Are you doing this alone?

Right now, yes. But not for long. The Certified Facilitator Program is how we scale without hiring. Facilitators operate as independent licensees. A VA handles social media from Month 4. A video editor takes over editing from Month 7. By Year 2, the business runs on a founder + contractors + licensees structure, not employees.

What happens if you burn out?

That is the right question. The honest answer: if I am the only one who can deliver BAM, the business has no future. That is exactly why we designed the Certified Facilitator Program from day one. By Month 18, we aim to have ten licensed facilitators operating independently. If I step back for a month, the business continues. The Ethics Charter and clinical supervision structure ensure quality holds even when I am not in the room.

How do you source animals and materials?

Closed-loop, ethically sourced. Limestone and slate are collected from local landscape yards as broken fragments (often free). Driftwood is foraged from beaches and forests with permission. Leaf litter is sterilized from clean public parks. Live animals come from a small, vetted network of captive breeders. We do not work with wild-caught specimens or mass-breeding facilities. We maintain three vetted breeders for every targeted genetic line.

Are you a medical therapy?

No. We are a peer-supported wellness practice. We do not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Every video, product, consultation, and page includes a clear Wellness Disclaimer. Our Ethics Charter is public. We do not interview people in active addiction or crisis. Every clinical pilot is conducted in partnership with licensed providers, never as a standalone intervention. This keeps us outside the rules that apply to licensed medical practice.

What insurance do you carry?

Commercial General Liability ($2M–$5M), an Exotic Wildlife Exhibition Rider for expos, and Professional Liability (Errors & Omissions). Home-based business liability is added once we do more than 10 on-site tank setups per year. Every vendor at every BAM expo signs a hold-harmless agreement assuming full liability for their own animals.

What evidence do you have that BAM works?

The framework is built on published research: Attention Restoration Theory (Kaplan), biophilia (Wilson), tactile grounding (Kappes & Morewedge), and perceptual revision (Doidge, Beck). Our own clinical pilot with a community partner is underway. Pre- and post-anxiety data are being collected using the GAD-7 scale. A white paper will be published within 12 months of the pilot's completion.

How much funding do you need?

$0 in equity. We are bootstrapped. We are seeking non-dilutive grants to accelerate: Starter Company Plus ($5,000) for product development and marketing, Ontario Trillium Foundation ($10K–$25K) for clinical pilot expansion, and Canada Summer Jobs (wage subsidy) for a student expos assistant. Total grant target: $40,000 over 24 months.

What is your moat? How do you stop copycats?

Three layers. First, proprietary terminology (trademarked). Second, our Anchor Club community creates social lock-in through accountability partners and streak tracking. Third, the Certified Facilitator network becomes a distribution moat — once facilitators are licensed and operating, competitors cannot easily replace them. And finally, our zero-cost supply chain means competitors cannot match our pricing.

What is the exit path?

Three options. (1) Acquisition by a large wellness or pet company entering the bio-architecture space (PetSmart, Chewy, Mars Petcare). (2) Acquisition by a corporate wellness provider seeking physical hardware (LifeSpeak, CoreHealth). (3) Independent operation as a profitable, founder-owned business with a charity arm. We are building for option 3 — with options 1 and 2 as upside.

What if a big player copies you?

They cannot copy the method because the method requires trust, community, and clinical credibility — not the resources they have. If a large player wants to enter the category, the fastest path is to license our method. That is exactly what our B2B licensing arm is designed for. We would rather supply the category than fight for it.
