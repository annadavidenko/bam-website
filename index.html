The Framework

From the Window of Tolerance to Predictive Coherence™

BAM operates on modern, evidence-based frameworks. It is grounded in Predictive Processing and Interoception, moving past outdated autonomic models.

1. The Window of Tolerance

Chronic stress dysregulates the nervous system, shrinking its capacity for arousal. When pushed outside this "window," the system defaults to hyper-arousal (panic) or hypo-arousal (dissociation). BAM protocols are engineered to mechanically widen this operational window.

2. Ecological Interoceptive Therapy™ (E.I.T.)

Our clinical methodology. By maintaining a living ecosystem, the user trains the brain to accurately process internal signals (interoception). The predictable rhythm of the biological system co-regulates the autonomic nervous system.

The Target State: Predictive Coherence™

When the brain's predictive models (what it expects to feel) align with the sensory evidence (safety, biological rhythm, nature), the threat system powers down. The result is Predictive Coherence™—absolute physiological stability and grounded presence.

Protocol 01

Observe

Soft attention on the ecosystem's movement, colour, and breath. This anchors the predictive brain in the present moment, interrupting rumination loops.

Protocol 02

Tend

Mist, feed, remove organic matter. A predictable, low-dopamine protocol of physical care. This builds interoceptive agency and structural control.

Protocol 03

Integrate

The external environment begins to mirror the internal terrarium. The nervous system generalizes safety to the natural world, expanding physiological independence.
