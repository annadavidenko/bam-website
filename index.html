🪴 What is the Biophilic Anchor Method (BAM)?

BAM is a peer‑supported wellness practice that uses small, manageable living ecosystems – called Biophilic Pets – for daily grounding, emotional regulation, and inner balance.

Created by a forensic intelligence professional in addiction recovery, BAM is not therapy. It is a daily 5‑minute practice of observation, gentle tending, and community sharing. The method rewires fear and disgust responses through perceptual revision, builds self‑efficacy, and provides a low‑dopamine alternative to addictive loops.

🧘 The BAM Method (5 min/day)

🪴 What is a Biophilic Pet?

A Biophilic Pet can be a moss jar, isopod culture, shrimp tank, calm snake, or even a polished Anchor Stone – anything that brings you into gentle, present‑moment contact with a living system.

🌿 Moss Jar
Sealed ecosystem, springtails – zero maintenance
⛰️ Anchor Stone

🐍 Calm Snake
