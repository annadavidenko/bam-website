Wellness has a delivery problem. BAM solves it with hardware.

Investor FAQ

Common Questions

What is your moat?

Three layers: (1) proprietary terminology and methodology (trademark pending), (2) the Anchor Club community lock-in with accountability pairs and streak tracking, and (3) the founder's lived recovery story and forensic intelligence background — which cannot be replicated. Additionally, we operate a zero-cost supply chain (local landscape yards, foraged materials) that competitors cannot match on cost.

How do you avoid regulatory risk in wellness?

We are explicitly not a clinical therapy. We are a peer-supported wellness practice. Every video, product, and consultation includes a Wellness Disclaimer. We do not diagnose, treat, or cure. We do not interview active addiction or suicidal participants. Our Ethics Charter is public and enforced. This keeps us outside the regulatory scope of licensed medical practice.

What are the unit economics?

Physical products carry 80–97% gross margins. Anchor Stones cost $0.30 and retail for $12. Desktop Sanctuary Jars cost $6–8 and retail for $35–45. Consultations are $75 with near-zero marginal cost. The Anchor Club subscription is $8/month with 90%+ margin. Expos net ~$5,850 per event with zero upfront capital.

Why a hybrid for-profit + charity structure?

It unlocks three things: (1) grants that a for-profit cannot access, (2) tax-deductible donations from wealthy donors (including appreciated securities with zero capital gains tax), and (3) institutional credibility with clinical partners. The for-profit arm pays salaries and scales commercially. The charity arm runs mission-driven programs and receives non-dilutive funding.

What is the exit path?

Three options: (1) acquisition by a large wellness or pet industry player (e.g., PetSmart, Chewy, Mars Petcare) seeking to enter the bio-architectural wellness category, (2) acquisition by a corporate wellness provider (e.g., LifeSpeak, CoreHealth) seeking physical hardware IP, or (3) sustained independent operation as a cash-flow-positive, founder-owned business with a charity arm. We are building for option 3 — with options 1 and 2 as upside.
