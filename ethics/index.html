Our Commitments

Ethics & Safety Charter

The Biophilic Anchor Method™ and Ecological Interoceptive Therapy™ are peer-supported wellness frameworks. We do not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Our clinical partnerships, community programs, and digital products are built on a foundation of strict ethical boundaries, human safety, and animal welfare.

The Charter

Six Pillars of Integrity

This charter governs all operations of Karma Strategic Intelligence Inc., the Biophilic Anchor Foundation, and all Certified B.A.M. Wellness Architects. Adherence to these principles is non-negotiable.

01. No Active Crisis Intervention

We do not interview, enrol, or treat individuals in active addiction, acute psychiatric crisis, or active suicidal ideation. BAM is a complementary practice for the stabilization and maintenance phases of recovery, or for high-stress professionals seeking preventative regulation. We are not an emergency service.

02. Autonomy and Informed Choice

Every participant must complete the EcoPet Suitability Questionnaire prior to enrolment. If the data indicates that a living ecosystem is not a match for their baseline, psychological triggers, or physical environment, we do not force the intervention. Complete autonomous choice and the right to withdraw at any time are built into the protocol.

03. Animal Welfare & The Five Freedoms

We operate a closed-loop, ethical supply chain. Animals are sourced exclusively from vetted captive breeders. We do not work with wild-caught specimens or mass-breeding facilities. We strictly adhere to the Five Freedoms of animal welfare.

  • Freedom from hunger and thirst
  • Freedom from discomfort
  • Freedom from pain, injury, or disease
  • Freedom to express normal behavior
  • Freedom from fear and distress

04. Human Safety & Hygiene Protocols

Interacting with living ecosystems carries inherent biological risks (e.g., naturally occurring bacteria like Salmonella). We mandate strict hygiene protocols for all participants and clinical partners.

  • Hand sanitization before and after handling any animal or substrate.
  • No kissing or placing animals near the face.
  • Immediate cleaning of any enclosure surfaces.
  • Clear warnings provided regarding allergies and immune-compromised individuals.

05. Clinical Oversight & Data Privacy

When operating within clinical or rehabilitation settings, BAM acts strictly as an adjunct to licensed care. Clinical staff retain full oversight of the patient's care. We do not provide medical advice or diagnosis.

Data Collection: We collect evaluation data using standardised tools (GAD-7 for anxiety, MAIA-2 for interoceptive awareness). All data is anonymized, stored securely in compliance with PIPEDA (Canadian privacy law), and only collected with explicit, written patient consent.

06. Framework Integrity & IP Protection

To ensure client safety and scientific standardization, the Biophilic Anchor Method™ must be delivered exactly as designed. Practitioners and Certified Wellness Architects are prohibited from altering the core protocols, terminology, or clinical frameworks. The methodology is legally protected (CIPO Reg. No. 1251262) to prevent exploitation and maintain the highest standard of care.

Questions About Our Ethics?

Transparency is one of our core pillars. If you are a clinician, partner, or participant and have questions about our safety protocols or animal welfare standards, please reach out.
